Updated September 28, 2026

How do I play?

Drag a cube face to turn a layer. Drag the background or move two fingers together to orbit. Pinch to zoom. Use the introduction and growth challenges for guided practice.

What does Full Journey unlock?

One purchase unlocks two worlds with 24 challenges in total, including two free samples, and the Dawn and Firefly themes. This is not a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open settings, choose Unlock Pro or Full Journey, and choose Restore purchase. Use the Apple Account that originally bought the product. An internet connection may be required.

Will my progress move to another device?

Progress and saves are local; Quietwist does not provide its own cloud sync. A restored purchase unlocks content but does not restore your game progress.

How do I report a problem?

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, the steps to reproduce the problem, and an optional screenshot. Never include account passwords or payment details.

如何操作魔方？

拖动魔方面可以转动一层；拖动背景或双指移动可以旋转视角，双指捏合可以缩放。入门引导与成长挑战提供练习。

Full Journey 解锁什么？

一次购买解锁两个世界、共 24 道挑战（含两道免费样例），以及 Dawn 和 Firefly 两款主题，不是订阅。

如何恢复购买？

打开设置，选择解锁 Pro 或 Full Journey，选择恢复购买。使用原购买所用的 Apple 账号，恢复时可能需要联网。

游戏进度能换设备吗？

进度与存档保存在本机，Quietwist 不提供自己的云同步。恢复购买会解锁内容，但不会恢复游戏进度。

如何反馈问题？

请提供设备型号、iOS 版本、应用版本、复现步骤以及可选截图。请勿提供账号密码或支付信息。
