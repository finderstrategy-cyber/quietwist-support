Privacy policy

Updated September 28, 2026

Your game stays on your device

Quietwist stores language and appearance settings, saved games, puzzle progress, and daily results on your device. It does not provide an app account or a developer-operated cloud sync service. Deleting the app or its data may remove these records.

Optional playtest notes

If you enable playtest recording in settings, gameplay events and local visit identifiers are stored on your device. Recording can be turned off in settings. Reports are not automatically sent to us; copying a report places it on your clipboard, and sharing it is your choice.

Purchases

Full Journey is a one-time Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment; Quietwist reads verified StoreKit transactions to unlock content and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and tracking

This version does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking. Game records are not transmitted to a developer server. Fonts and game resources are bundled for offline use.

Support requests

If you contact us yourself, the information you choose to include is used to answer your request. Avoid sending passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information.

Deleting local data

To remove local game records, delete the app and its data using device settings. Offloading the app may preserve its data. Deleting local data does not cancel or refund an Apple purchase.

隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 9 月 28 日

游戏数据保存在本机

Quietwist 在你的设备上保存语言与外观设置、游戏存档、谜题进度和每日完成记录。应用没有登录账号，也没有开发者提供的云同步服务。删除应用或应用数据可能清除这些记录。

自愿开启的试玩记录

如果在设置中开启试玩记录，游戏事件与本机访问标识会保存在设备上，可随时在设置中关闭记录。报告不会自动发送给我们；复制报告会将其放入剪贴板，是否进一步分享由你决定。

应用内购买

Full Journey 是通过 Apple 完成的一次性内购。Apple 处理付款，Quietwist 读取经过验证的 StoreKit 交易来解锁内容和恢复购买。我们不会收到你的银行卡信息。Apple 按其自身隐私政策处理购买信息。

广告与跟踪

当前版本没有广告、第三方分析或跟踪。游戏记录不会上传至开发者服务器；字体和游戏资源随应用打包，可离线使用。

主动联系支持

如果你主动联系我们，我们使用你自愿提供的信息来处理问题。请勿发送密码、支付信息或不必要的个人资料。

删除本机数据

可在设备设置中删除应用及其数据，以移除本机游戏记录。“卸载 App”可能保留数据；删除本机数据不会取消或退款 Apple 购买。
